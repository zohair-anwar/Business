The job leaves a mark. Carrying it shouldn't be the job too.

Cumulative exposure

The calls add up. Repeated exposure to trauma and critical incidents takes a measurable toll over a career — long before anyone calls it a crisis.

A culture of "push through"

Stigma, fear for your career, and not wanting to burden the people at home keep too many from reaching out until things break.

Generic help misses the mark

A counsellor who's never had to understand a night-shift rotation, a use-of-force review, or what a "bad call" really means starts ten steps behind.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really confidential?

Yes. Off Watch Wellness is independent — we're not operated by any police service, agency, or employer. What you discuss with your clinician is protected by the same professional and legal standards as any registered counselling or psychology practice in Canada. We will never report your participation to your employer.

Is this only for police and RCMP?

No. We're built around the public-safety community broadly — police, RCMP, firefighters, paramedics and EMS, corrections officers, 911 dispatchers, and the families who carry the job alongside them. Police and RCMP are a core focus because of how this started, but you're welcome here.

Will you work with my benefits or coverage?

That's the plan. Many first responders have extended health benefits or employer/association programs that cover mental-health care. As we launch, we're prioritizing being compatible with the coverage you already have. We'll share specifics with waitlist members first.

Virtual or in person?

Both, where we can. Secure virtual sessions make it possible to get matched with the right clinician regardless of where you're posted, and we're working toward in-person options in key regions.

What does it cost?

Pricing will be transparent and in line with standard counselling and psychology rates in your province, with the goal of maximizing what your benefits cover. Waitlist members will get full details before anything begins — joining the list costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

What if I need help right now?

Please don't wait on us. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7) or call 911 in an emergency. Off Watch Wellness is a counselling service in development and is not set up for crisis or emergency response.
